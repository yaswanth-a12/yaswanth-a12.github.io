Solar Water Audit

An Android app for field engineers who inspect solar-powered rural water supply schemes and audit water-testing laboratories. It replaces paper forms: answers, measurements, the location of the water source and photographs are recorded on the phone, even without a network, and sent to Google Sheets when the phone is online.

Solar Water Audit is not a public app. Pravayan EcoTech Private Limited gives it to its field staff and authorised project personnel, and the project administrator approves every person before they can sign in and send data. It is not listed on Google Play.

What the app does

Two structured forms

A scheme form (solar panels, controller, borewell and pumps, discharge test, tank and network, water quality, operation, repairs, governance) and a laboratory audit form.

Works offline

Every answer is saved on the phone as it is typed. Drafts can be resumed and nothing has to be sent from the field.

Checked answers

Units, allowed ranges, skip instructions and NA / NK options are built into each question, and a review screen lists anything still to fix.

Source location on request

The position of the borewell is read from the phone's GPS only when the engineer taps the capture button. There is no tracking and no background location.

Photo checklist

Photographs of the signboard, panels, controller, wellhead, tank, supply points and registers, named after the scheme ID.

Google Sheets synchronisation

Records go to a spreadsheet, and photos to a folder, in the engineer's own Google Drive. Both are shared with the project administrator. Each record carries an ID, so it is never added twice.

How access works

  1. The field engineer installs the app supplied by the project team.
  2. The engineer sends an access request from the app. The project administrator receives it by e-mail and approves or rejects it.
  3. After approval, the engineer signs in with Google and the app creates the engineer's project spreadsheet and photo folder in their Google Drive and shares them with the administrator.
  4. The administrator can withdraw access at any time. Records that were already sent stay with the project.

How the app uses your Google account

Sign-in uses Google's own screens, so the app never sees your Google password. The app asks Google only for your name and e-mail address and for the Google Drive permission drive.file, which covers only the files that the app creates or that you open with it. The app cannot see or change your other Drive files, and it does not use Gmail, Contacts or Calendar.

The app has no advertising and no analytics, and data is never sold. The Privacy Policy explains in full what is collected, why, where it is stored and who can see it.
