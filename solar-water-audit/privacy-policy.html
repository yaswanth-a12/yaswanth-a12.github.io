Privacy Policy

Solar Water Audit (Android app and its access-approval service) · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Solar Water Audit app collects, how it is used, where it is stored and who can see it, including information that the app receives from Google APIs. The app is provided by Pravayan EcoTech Private Limited ("we", "us").

In short

  • The app is used only by field staff whom the project administrator has approved.
  • You sign in with Google. The app asks only for your name and e-mail address and for the Google Drive permission drive.file, which covers only the files the app creates or that you open with it.
  • Your records and photographs are kept on your phone and in a spreadsheet and photo folder in your own Google Drive, which the app shares with the project administrator.
  • Location is read only when you tap the button that captures a water source's position. There is no background location and no tracking.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics, and we never sell data.

1. Who we are

Pravayan EcoTech Private Limited provides the Solar Water Audit app and runs the service that handles access requests. We decide how the information described here is used. You can reach us at yaswanth_a@wr.iitr.ac.in.

2. Who uses the app

The app is a work tool for field engineers and other authorised personnel who inspect rural water supply schemes and audit water-testing laboratories. It is not offered to the public and is not intended for children. A person can send data only after the project administrator approves their access request.

3. Information the app handles

Your name and e-mail address From Google when you sign in with Google To identify you, check that your access is approved, and show which account your records go to On your phone, and in the project's access list

The forms do not ask for the names of households or other community members, and you should not enter them. Questions put to households record their answers only.

The app uses the phone's location only while you are capturing a position, and it asks for the location permission for that purpose alone. It does not collect contacts, messages, call logs, files outside the app, or advertising identifiers, and it does not send notifications.

4. Google user data

When you sign in, the app asks for these Google permissions:

  • Basic sign-in (openid, email, profile): your name and e-mail address, used as described in section 3. The sign-in token is also sent to our access-approval service, which checks it with Google to confirm which account is making the request.
  • Google Drive, files used with this app only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): used to create your project spreadsheet and photo folder, write your records and upload your photographs to them, read the list of schemes that the administrator places in your spreadsheet, and share the spreadsheet and the photo folder with the project administrator.

With these permissions the app cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Google Drive, and it does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service. Your Google password is entered only on Google's own screens; the app never sees it and does not store access tokens.

We use Google user data only to provide these features of the app. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to build profiles of you, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. We transfer it only as described in section 5: to the project administrator and project team as part of the app's purpose, to Google as the provider of the services involved, or when the law requires it. Apart from you, only the project administrator and the project team who work with the project records can read them.

Solar Water Audit's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Who receives information

  • The project administrator receives your access request by e-mail, keeps the project's access list, has edit access to your project spreadsheet and photo folder, and keeps a master copy of the records from all approved spreadsheets, updated about every hour.
  • The project team analyses the records and prepares the project reports, which present results by scheme, by laboratory or in summary form.
  • Google stores the spreadsheets and photographs in Google Drive and Google Sheets, runs the access-approval service on Google Apps Script and sends its e-mails. Google handles this information under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap: if you open the optional map preview, the app downloads map images from tile.openstreetmap.org, run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. These requests reveal your phone's IP address and the map area around the captured position. You can turn the map preview off in the app's Settings, and positions never need the map.
  • Authorities, only when the law or a valid legal order requires us to disclose information.

We do not sell, rent or trade information, and we do not share it for advertising.

6. Storage and security

  • On the phone, records and photographs are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read and which is excluded from Android cloud backups.
  • Records are sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to Google. The app sends waiting records in the background only when the phone is online.
  • Only accounts that the administrator has approved can send data, and the administrator can withdraw access at any time.
  • The app does not let you delete a record that has not been sent yet. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on the phone, including records that have not been sent, so send your records first.

7. How long information is kept

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the app.
  • In your Google Drive: your project spreadsheet and photo folder stay until you or the administrator delete them.
  • Administrator's access list and master copy: for as long as the project needs them to complete its work and reports, and for any period our contracts or the law require. After that they are deleted or archived.

8. Your choices

  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account under Security, "Your connections to third-party apps and services" (myaccount.google.com/connections). After that, the app can no longer write to your Drive.
  • Location: you can refuse or withdraw the location permission in Android settings and still fill the other questions.
  • Map preview: you can switch it off in the app's Settings.
  • Access, correction and deletion: write to us to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. Some project records may need to be kept so that the project's results stay complete; if so, we will tell you which records and why.

9. Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. We will tell approved users about important changes in the app or by e-mail before they take effect.
